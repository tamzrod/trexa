## Justification Summary

1. **AI-First Score**: 8.75/10 - highest among evaluated styling approaches

3. **Consistent Naming**: Convention-based, AI can learn patterns
4. **Design System**: Built-in spacing, colors, typography
5. **Tree Shaking**: Only used classes in final bundle
6. **Component Libraries**: Compatible with shadcn/ui, Headless UI
