## Justification Summary

1. **Engineering Semantic**: Colors carry engineering meaning (voltage, state)
2. **WCAG Compliant**: All colors meet 4.5:1 minimum contrast
3. **Color Blind Safe**: Every status color has non-color indicator
4. **Theme Compatible**: Works with Light + Dark + High Contrast
5. **Tailwind CSS**: Integrates with TDR-006

## Accessibility Rule

**RULE**: Every color used to convey status MUST have a non-color indicator:
- Shape differentiation
- Pattern fills
- Icon overlays
- Text labels
- Animation effects
