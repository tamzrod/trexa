## Human Intent (AUTHORITATIVE)

> Build a visual engineering platform where users can create engineering diagrams using a WYSIWYG interface.
> 
> The platform shall support multiple engineering domains from a common architecture.
> 
> Initial supported domains: Single Line Diagram (SLD), Geographic Information System (GIS)
> 
> Future domains may include: P&ID, SCADA, Protection, Process Engineering, Others
> 
> The platform shall support drag-and-drop engineering objects, connection points, engineering relationships, and reusable rendering technologies such as SVG, Apache ECharts, or other future renderers.

**Note**: This intent is authoritative. The laboratory shall NOT redefine or replace it.
