## Investigation Objective

Determine whether the term **WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get)** should remain part of Trexa's product positioning or be replaced with terminology that better reflects Trexa's long-term vision as a visual engineering platform.

**Final Determination**: WYSIWYG should be removed from product positioning.

---

## Background

Trexa is evolving into a visual engineering platform capable of supporting multiple engineering domains with semantic understanding, AI assistance, engineering validation, and domain-aware modeling.

The current application documentation describes Trexa as a WYSIWYG engineering editor. There is concern that the term "WYSIWYG" may position Trexa as a generic drawing application rather than an engineering modeling platform.

This investigation evaluates whether retaining or removing the term better supports Trexa's long-term vision.
