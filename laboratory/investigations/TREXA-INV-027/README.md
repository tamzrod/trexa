## Quick Summary

This investigation evaluated whether the term "WYSIWYG" should remain part of Trexa's product positioning or be replaced with terminology that better reflects Trexa's long-term vision as a visual engineering platform.

**Recommendation**: Remove WYSIWYG and replace with "Visual Engineering Platform"

## Key Findings

1. **Architecture Alignment**: WYSIWYG covers only 20% of Trexa's architecture
2. **Vision Alignment**: WYSIWYG supports only 1 of 5 vision pillars
3. **Capabilities Coverage**: Only 15% of platform capabilities are WYSIWYG-related
4. **Future Coverage**: WYSIWYG covers 0% of future roadmap capabilities
5. **Competitive Parity**: Professional engineering tools never use WYSIWYG

## Recommendation

**Remove WYSIWYG** from all product positioning and replace with **"Visual Engineering Platform"**

This change:
- Is architecturally accurate
- Aligns with Trexa's vision
- Differentiates from generic diagramming tools
- Is future-proof for multi-domain expansion
- Sets correct user expectations
