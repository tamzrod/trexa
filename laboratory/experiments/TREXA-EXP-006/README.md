# TREXA-EXP-006: Plant Growth Under Light Conditions

**Status**: IN_PROGRESS

## Overview

This experiment tests the hypothesis that plants grow faster when exposed to sunlight compared to plants kept in complete darkness.

## Quick Summary

- **Hypothesis**: Plants grow faster in sunlight than darkness
- **Method**: 14-day controlled experiment
- **Subjects**: Bean seedlings (10 total, 5 per group)
- **Variables**: Sunlight vs complete darkness
