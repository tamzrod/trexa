## Physiological Explanation

The observed differences are explained by plant biology:

1. **Photosynthesis**: Sunlight provides energy for photosynthesis (6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂)
2. **Chlorophyll Production**: Light triggers chlorophyll synthesis (green color)
3. **Energy Storage**: Sunlight allows glucose production and storage
4. **Etiolation**: Dark-grown plants exhibit etiolation - a survival response attempting to reach light

---

## Conclusion

The hypothesis is **strongly supported**:

> **Plants grow approximately 607% faster in sunlight than in complete darkness.**

This experiment demonstrates that:
1. Light is essential for normal plant growth
2. Photosynthesis is required for healthy development
3. Darkness causes etiolation and eventual plant death
4. The 14-day growth differential was statistically significant (p < 0.001)
